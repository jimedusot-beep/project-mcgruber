# Pop-Up Surprise Box: reproducible build guide

**Status:** mechanical version proposed; no build verified. These are proposed steps; no hardware test is implied.

## Preconditions

Read [safety](../../docs/SAFETY.md). Identify actual supply voltage/current, connector pins, switch contact state and motor/load ratings; do not use unverified parts under power. See [BOM](BOM.csv).

## Procedure

1. Measure available cardboard box and figure weight.
2. Fold an accordion paper spring; avoid hard or sharp launching parts.
3. Construct hinge and simple low-force latch.
4. Perform 20 manual cycles and record any jams.
5. Only add actuation after physical mechanics work.

## Pass gate

- [ ] Specific part models and safe ratings recorded.
- [ ] Repeatable observations measured and logged, including failed trials.
- [ ] Real images/video captured without personal data.
- [ ] Any proposed mechanical fit actually measured and tested.
