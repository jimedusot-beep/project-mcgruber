McGruber Circuit Lab · P04 Object-Removal Alarm

Switch continuity bench

Map salvaged mouse-switch contacts using your Fluke. This is a worksheet, not a digital meter: only readings you enter are analyzed.

Before measuring: Unplug USB and disconnect every battery and power supply. Use continuity/ohms ONLY on a completely unpowered, isolated switch. Other circuits attached to the PCB can give misleading results. Do not apply 9V to an Arduino GPIO.

1 · Identify the switch

Photograph and label the physical legs A, B and (if present) C. Do not assume A is COM. Check each pair while the switch is released and pressed.

3 · Repeat the observation

Check a full press/release for each cycle. Mark a cycle only if all contact states matched your mapped readings. Slow continuity checks do not quantify fast contact bounce.

0 of 10 consistent cycles reported.
