# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Inventory and simple prototypes
1. Record inventory and photograph part numbers.
2. Continuity test mouse switches with all power disconnected.
3. Prototype mechanical surprise box and switch-triggered LED.

## Phase 2: Independent alarms
4. Verify buzzer and regulated supply ratings.
5. Build object-removal audible alarm.
6. Build standalone comparator-based visible tripwire.

## Phase 3: Modular integration
7. Camera motion-to-sound software prototype.
8. Verify key fob encoding and compatible receiver.
9. Test matched IR break-beam pair.
10. Integrate sensors and outputs in a reusable box.

## Phase 4: Robotics
11. Diagnose Elmo donor hardware and document motor/speaker tests.
12. Build independently controlled talking and moving character.

Do not mark phases complete until an experiment report documents repeatable results.
