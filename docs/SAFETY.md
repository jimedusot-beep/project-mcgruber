# Safety and validation

- Use isolated low-voltage DC supplies, and never use mains wiring in first prototypes.
- Disconnect power before checking switch continuity or resistance.
- Confirm supply voltage, polarity, LED current limit, connector pinout and output load current from an actual datasheet/measurement.
- Protect inductive motor/relay circuits with appropriate drivers and suppression.
- Do not feed 12V from a key fob into 3.3V/5V microelectronics.
- Use a current-limited supply where practical. Inspect salvaged lithium cells for damage; don't reuse damaged cells.
- Infrared can be invisible: never aim high-power IR or lasers at eyes; visible LED emitters are preferred for initial demonstrations.
- Avoid startling someone near stairs, roads, fragile equipment or with a foreseeable injury risk.
- Record test results; proposed circuits and unverified ratings are not fabrication instructions.
