# Minimal photo and video capture checklist

For each invention, take:
1. **Parts layout photo** including labels but excluding private identifiers.
2. **Wiring overview photo**, unpowered and well illuminated.
3. **Close-up** of any pinout and meter measurement screen, with ranges/units legible.
4. **Short uncut functional clip** showing idle state, trigger and output at least twice.
5. **Test log** matching date, project revision, source and exact setup.

Crop/blur private addresses, faces, license plates, serial numbers and screens showing secrets before public release, while keeping electrical evidence unaltered. No fake photos or videos should be added to a verification record.
