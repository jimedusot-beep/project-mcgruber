# 3D printing — sources to study

Links are **learning references, not endorsements, file imports or evidence of successful McGruber prints**.

| Source | Learn from it | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenSCAD documentation](https://openscad.org/documentation.html) | Free parametric 3D CAD, changing wall thickness and clearance | A source file is automatically printable |
| [OpenSCAD User Manual](https://files.openscad.org/documentation/manual/OpenSCAD_User_Manual.html) | Difference operations, parametric solids and export | Compiling a mesh proves real-world fit |
| [FreeCAD Part Design tutorial](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Creating_a_simple_part_with_PartDesign.md) | Sketches, dimensions, pad and pocket | An imported mesh becomes editable CAD history |
| [PrusaSlicer documentation](https://help.prusa3d.com/category/prusaslicer_204) | Print orientation, supports and material estimates | Filament estimates include every real-world cost |
| [Printables model catalog](https://www.printables.com/) | Search for tested mounts, cases and fit coupons | Every model is open-source or free to redistribute |
| [Printables licensing terms](https://www.prusa3d.com/page/terms-of-service-of-printables-com_231249/) | Makers choose from several licenses with different permissions | A model's platform availability grants universal rights |
| [McGruber source-study bench](../../docs/OPEN_SOURCE_STUDY_BENCH_2026-11-01.md) | Existing project-specific 3D and engineering research | External source success proves our donor hardware |

## Model reuse checklist

1. Record the **exact design page URL, author, model version, and license** in an idea's notes or issue.
2. Confirm download, modification, attribution and redistribution permissions for that particular file; different models on one site may have different rules.
3. Prefer linking to the source rather than copying someone else's STL.
4. If adapting, identify original work, changes, and required notices. Do not place third-party work under McGruber's hardware license unless the rights actually allow it.
5. Record whether a source has a real printed example, but do not treat that as testing *our* measurements, material or hardware.
